Hypnotic clockwork angel
I was faded tangled
I was slated to be be recycled
Floating towards the light deciple
Disheveled memories blended grotesque
The quest caught me off balance
Too much guess work in the callus
I fell victim to her tallons
Tumbling rung by rung
Sippin from the thimble
Severing limb from torso
Bleeding silver from my carapace

Alchemical marriages
Of poetic terrorists
A prisoner of infinity
Forced to eat my arrogance

No pain
No mystery untold
Holding
Precious threads woven into ropes

He looked me in the eyes and spoke
The last words that we ever heard
When I heard the news I almost broke
But I still dodged the curse
I know you touched the sun
And it burns in your daughter
And even if I couldn't stop the choke
I was still an honour
These wires caught these vessels tossed
Stormy seas but still we cross
You were me and I was lost
Lodestone take me home

I gather my magic
Gather my bones
