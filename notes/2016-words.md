day breaks on a planet, massive. compared to the ancient animals the current inhabitants are slight. but their edifices tower over their stature. statues of the ancestors and towers that attempt to scrape heaven are also dwarfed by the massive clouds of smoke that spew from the vents of factories.  On the surface, man struggles to  find an identity, singing into mountains hoping for an echo, cutting into trees older than his name, hoping to finally pay the debt that has been levered against him.  Oil is brought up from the deepest caverns, and atomized into the upper atmosphere after it’s energy has been unlocked and transfered.  in valleys overlooked and forgotten for now, a drummer mimics the sound of their heartbeat, the rythm of the seasons and stars.

winter snow.  the air crystallizes into prisms reflecting infinity.  my dreams strange worlds of meeting beings beyond the borders. dank warm rotten walls crawl with life, inside my core fermentations  activate. being free beyond control as barriers disintegrate into purgatory. I cross the abyss fearless, peerless. I am me, unto myself i am a self contained cycle of awareness unburdened.  hardened crosses melting into loops, reforming into new shapes yet unseen. the green leaves are just ideas. but seeds still hold the sun under darkened blankets of snow. a foxes trail leads far from the broken path. the slow movement of a raft made still by the rivers slumber.  deep in the bottoms of swamps the creatures cling to the life needed to be born anew, remanifesting someday, the way not defined, but every step leaves a trace. the memory of sky, pink and blue like shining sunsets, sleep.  the morning arrives in splendor with silent trumpets hailing the un-assailable exctacy, allowing all possible channels to exist in non linear.  no paradox. free will is an illusion, but every choice made is massive, casting shadows so long they loop the planet and extend past every horizon.  the taste of flame, the chosen pane. the method is madness, but each teacher is still a student. I love. i rest in this unknowable sound, the vapor hangs over a speck of town.  people struggle, their hate the very thing that makes them human.  I am not human, or animal even, although my body’s fluids move, reminded of a stagnation deeper than stress, frozen warmth of a million cells hypothermic, the beauty of the end, to realize that it is but another beginning. this piece is of the now, textures deep as dream, no scheme but to express, the mouth my means. a bean contains a universe. this cursive script forming ellipses in literature, loops of thought. I am no budda on the road, i do not intend to die, but to sleep to find what dream may come. illusions strange to stir the heart. every sensation is but a single bead on this endless train. no gain or loss but an anamnesis of forgotten connections, like jung’s riverbeds the runes have always been singing the song of my blood. over mountains fought tooth and nail, rip bleeding scratches, canyons scar the sky,  at light speed my mass is infinite, dusk eats all the light and i grow over the room, my bed becomes the heavens, I fly with a new tilt, over desserts and tundra, jungles and seas, effortlessly i drift from room to room, spiraling inwards as my core expands ceaselessly. years and moments loose their context and become confused, but it is a confusion welcomed, like the unknown yielding of a pillow to my head. yawn like lions, satiated by the blood of hardened ideas who’s time has come. medicine in every breath, life in every rep. there is a shifting, the floor elongating and streaches away in dream, a waking, a falling into life. to let go of the tragidy of my existance and accept a million fates, the crucible bubbles slow, but the raven knows nothing ever can stop, just flow.

When you trick the most vulnerable, the hard masses follow.  the blood of a heart perforated by grief, still leads the wolves along it’s trail.  whether  they run to kill or console makes no difference to the lower mind.  to hold is to have, like a child who crushes a pet hamster out of love.  grief and joy are so similar, separated only by a thought.  we love to watch the death throes or the orgasms of once calm celebrity faces. when met with the downfall of a star, people race to be polarized. the ghost of their conviction leaps at the chance to finally divide itself fully from the deepest power, the power to change.  these programs run so deep, these scripts play for keeps.we gleefully abandon or selves , like an addict looking for any excuse for another hit, creating catastrophes at every corner to justify the strengthening of our dogma.  And those who know this, do they really know?  or is their relentless attacks on the innocent unconscious just late stage symptoms of the same imposed illness?  hearts hardened by knowledge. wisdom stolen. But who am i to judge? my parents taught me to be kind, to give of myself. now i have more love for these two dimensional characters propped up by thousands of years of slaughtered corpses than i do for my own soul.  Activists promote anger, to justify their own. they get off on the image of a mouse standing up to an elephant and trying to block it’s path. Understandably this is the epitome of stupidity, or was it bravery? another fine line whose border negotiations have claimed many lives before their natural span.  A malicious script created accidentally, we took the toaster apart to know, now we’ve forgotten what toast even tastes like. left with jams, butter and knives in our cupboard. now ancient artifacts that confuse us as we gnaw on the rusted mechanisms of our tools. To ascend, to watch from a high place. looking down your nose at the rushing masses with pity, perhaps or is it the joy mixed with sadness of a parent whose child has begun a long journey of learning to love.  taking our first steps we stumble and the earth shakes as we crater once again. magma splashes, the ocean produces tidal waves that destroy the cites in our toyboy.
Rusty gears and springs, washed away and rearranged, emerge from sunken islands and stir our ancient ancestral memories. the collective normalized murder, pathology, kill the ones that shine brightest before they find their place in the walk of stars. Or be a star martyr, supernova to warm the fingers of a fevered frame.  too hot, too cold, we blame the fire, the ice, the weather, never our own sickness even when the thermometer reads even, we feel these ghosts fly through our body, but we will not hear their names.
