I'm walking alone through an unfamiliar city
Copper cobblestones slope downwards towards a Harbour, a darkened ocean fading into distant obscurity
A sense of void and timelessness has enveloped my senses.
My hands reach out to touch something of substance
Trinkets in shop front distract me from my observations
A throbbing hum fills the motionless air
The windows are slowly melting with a dull fire
A reflection caught, my face is not my own
