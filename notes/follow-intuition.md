Follow intuition
Rules are for the weak
Words live in a tomb we speak
Questing for the best we reach

Resting in the ledge of kismet
Misfits but we found each other
Thunder clap collapse my raps
Enacting every sound is smothered

Mothers to the child that's hunted
Spawn across the aeons touching
Colors that with which we wrap each other
Spill from us like sap

Flower from the wounded earth
Rose's in the ditch uncovered
Rose above the ground I hovered
All relations blood and lovers

Deeper than the waters moving
Crucial food and fluid proof
Span the distance bridge the grid with
Senses let us know the truth

Look beyond the tomb
Out side the room
the starry night
The part of us that seeks the light
The shadow calls us deeper flight

Dive beneath your covers
Hide your face and incubate
I am eye to eye with butterflies
Transforming is my fate.

I aligned my compass
Arrow from the bow
My heart has sown
Seeds I reap the winds have blown

Circumnavigated the globe

Polar vortex
Virtuous virus
Mutated stylus
Crunch the wildest numbers
Finding the mileage
For the nihilist

Keep the tractor trucking through this mud that we call life.
The battle it will rattle you
Walking on the sharpest knife

Bayonets and epitaphs
The walls on which the prophets wrote
The trumpets crumble Jericho
The larynx holds the weirding note

Resonances echoing

The path is beckoning

These days we face a reckoning

The quickening the pestilence
