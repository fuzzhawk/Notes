A moment of growth in an age of stagnation
Phase canceled this current cages confines, climb higher into conundrum
Waveforms, peaks and valleys.
Slope of your senses. Contextual mixed messages.
As I orbit your presence
Gravity stretching my scope of control.
Reveal my illusion
This trick of existence
I have lied my way past the gatekeepers
Into this ancient cadence with grace of hypnosis
Kissed by eristic missiles gifted from mutant minstrels.
Medieval thoughtforms on their path to eminence
Innocence leading ignorance
Through engines of misanthropey
In a century who can say what their dance will be
We are first person puzzle pieces searching for cursed fleeces
The dragon that guards the temple is asleep and amnesiac
We have seen the act that follows in our self indulgent fictions
A world where meaning has been melted down and recast
And perhaps we are the last
What does that make our meeting then
You sung me into existence
But I resisted your system
Now my nerves are synthetic and my organs are pistons
