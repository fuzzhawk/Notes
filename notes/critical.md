Ideas into the blast furnace
Castaway on a flame drifted
Decoupled from meaning
The exact never existed

The mind a muscle clenched
Around a figment
Fist open to reveal
only the imprint of a signet

The brain like clay impressionable
Molded into meaning
Further from the shore it warps the form the tide receding

Critical thinking

The city superimposed on the animal channels

My tarnished vocabulary flickering like a thousand candles
