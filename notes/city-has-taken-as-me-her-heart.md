A light rain falls on buildings grit making the city shine in its own nightlight
The strange giant silloettes of industrial complex take on the form of distant slumbering beasts, occasional ripples cast over their muscular bodies by the headlights of passing traffic.
I step over cobblestones and dark rushing gutters, though alleys decorated with plumes of steam and wires sagging heavy  with the energy of information.
I am the only one awake somehow in this midnight city.  I know of a place where there are others like me, a hidden corner lit by neon, a place I will know by the throbbing pulse of arcane synthesis and the small groups of shadow folk with shining eyes, some with cigarettes sending small clouds up to meet the falling rain.  But for the moment i am alone, the city has taken me as her heart. My footsteps beating out a rhythm as I move, always at the centre, my eyes carving unconscious hieroglyphs into the towering constructs above me.
Amd In this half light, at this secret hour, the city seems like something not made by human hands. An alien crystallization formed when some ancient meteor collided with the earth, a never before seen chemical reaction sending spires of shining metal and stone reaching for the space behind the stars.
