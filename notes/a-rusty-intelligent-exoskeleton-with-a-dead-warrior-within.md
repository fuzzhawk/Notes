A rusty intelligent exoskeleton with a dead warrior within lives in an overgrown battlefield.  Skeletons of soldiers and crows are his companions.  He spends his time in contemplation of the sword that killed him in battle
A kid and their friend from the village nearby befriends the ai controlling the downed mecha suit
They find a way to reactivate the mech but the friend gets dusted with nano material that begins to transform them into a warrior. The friend takes the haunted sword and the kid learns how to pilot the mech and they set off on an adventure
