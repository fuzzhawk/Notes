Dark moon
Water rising
Reflected horizon
Curve of your smile
Sword of your style

Breath deep under cover
A nest and a lover
You walked into my life
lost in the twilight

Wide eyed and wandering
I was an invisible person
You saw through my disguise
Spied another version

Murmur
Mirror
