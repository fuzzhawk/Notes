Future earth
The surface irradiated and polluted.
Giant scrubbers and desalinization plants on the horizon. Also giant mining equipment
Underground breeding and cloning facilities create workers who repair the machines until the time they succumb to the toxic environment
Some workers escape and have created a small enclave in a less polluted area
The rich live in city levels far above the largest oceans - massive orbital manufacturing ports and space elevators criss cross the sky.

Materials from all over the solar system and several nearby stars are brought  to earth to be remade into orbital infrastructure, ships and other items.  Trillions of tons of waste are produced every year the majority being radioactive and unstable isotopes and biohazardous material. The garbage planet originally functioned as a giant recycling facility and was one of humanities greatest achievements of engineering, but was choked with garbage shipments by the second year of its operation.  100 years later it had become a dumping point for colonies on other planets and also long distance generation ships returning from other star systems.
