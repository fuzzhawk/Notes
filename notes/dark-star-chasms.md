Drifting past consciousness to the unified field

Orbitally rearranged molecules spin miniscule past my optics

A dream of a bee on pomegranate seconds before awakening

Secret crystallization sending tendrils centerfugal

Enter suspension my story accelerates to the speed of aeons

Tension strung tightrope between chasms of creation

Presence beyond sentience, woven in

Vines of my stumbling jungle grow thickening

Waves crash washing past roots subterainian

Etched in acid, circuitry solving riddles unasked.

A vast Vista stretches beyond this wall of confusion

Breaking past cloud ceiling to a place unremembered

Presence of absolute lucidity

Timeless

Omniscient
