A restless mind journeys the worlds of night
Strange corridors
Collapsed elastic memory
Fingers snap
Her voice killing me instantly

A parade of judges
Ignorant in their hubris
Stand guard at each corner
Clutching rusting weapons

Malignant mimics
Entice from beyond the portal
Taking the shape of old friends, loved ones
but i've seen their true faces now
Twisted mockery of a human form
