Bless my single brain cell
I heard the rain fell
I heard the farm is well
I heard you talking in your sleep to calm yourself
You walked the the razors edge where so many slid
Use rope, roll for it. The goddess kept you guessing
You taught me more than any possession.
You and James were the ones that saved my presence. My omniscient message
Strange attractors bearing offerings
With prophecies of nonsense.
When I took the dive I kept my conscience
The aliens that kept so many haunted could not abscond with my belongings.

Watching your children grow has been a pleasure
And you taught me not to fear the weather.  Whenever it rains I can see those tears of joy
And when the thunder strikes I think of ordinance to be deployed.

Tis an Ill wind that blows no minds
Full
Before the sunrise

Conjure discordian borderline magic
Wreaking the havoc
One rep for leviathan massive
