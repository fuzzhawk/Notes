We quested to reveal
A door inside the world
Beneath the sea it boils
It beckons and unfurls

Blood curdles at the sight
To debauched for mortal eyes
Comprehension breaks inside
Where the moistened king resides

The smells of spices lost
Overtake your mind is washed away
Carrion upon the shore
The sunken crypt decays

In forms not known to man
The hidden legions hatch their plans
Beneath the waves they hide
In temples drowned their idols lie

Beneath the fathoms veil
Madness your mind assailed
Corrupt visages fade
But evil lives in still remains
