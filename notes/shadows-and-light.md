Shadows and light
Why do you shine
Holding it tight
Every time

When you try to find a way out
The exit lost and all surrounds you
With no one you can rely on
Thread you follow helps them find you

Weird ways
Slow days
Parallel
Folding

Battling my
Festering mind
Holding it tight
Only a ride

When I finally find the way out
I am right back there behind myself
Seeing clearly in the night
Who could know how far below we fell
