Need to write some stuff. Not grandiose things or maybe some. Not a diary but memories and observations are welcome. A story free from tethers, a waking dream
Collapsing within itself. I the word monster machine gobbling meaning, pixelating the world into cubes of definition. Caress of the interlaced lattice of amphibious picean forms. Amorphous objectives grasping to attach to a central node of action. Words people hear, need to have passion in performance. an inventory of convictions would be useful but is unattainable currently. Although a thought occurred, in all my fantastic fantasies of violence, the toxic rush of intrusive thought is extremely rarely if ever one of killing another, whether in defence or some other justification. Humbled by revelation, clumsy on page with the thousand distractions but inwards and onwards. Vocabulary sundered by dum dum brain mush. I'm falling asleep in myself, need a cleanse and a break. Deep in the mix overtones sounding out parallel spaces I. Resonant surface reflecting my adolescent awakening, in 1995 I would have been 10 most of the year , around 5 years later I would have discovered weed and trip hop. I remember making a beat on my parents computer in audiomultch that had a layered vocal chant, or moan even. And a sample from the band the tornadoes, slowed and reversed. This in conjunction with my experiences going deep into myself to massive attack were some of my first personally spiritual connections with music.
It was a channel of escape to a space exotic but familiar, certain overtones . Then I met
David prodan and was exposed to aphex and the warp/ninja tune world. Several time early on I found glimpses of those tones. One beat
I made unlocked an early freestyle flow. It had a reverb piano pad and a slowed down break beat from some pack on the internet. I would like to make this place more accessible to people, perhaps it's a useful space, or one that could inspire other kids to nerd out and become artists.and overtone, slight dissonance, a resonant body breathing in the room. Bowed, digital waves, struck tines and strings. Glass and stone and static, receiving sounds from the swarm to accompany the tragic now. Chasing tones before they blow up everything. Up on the clay cliffs, looking for omens. A Raven dropped a pice of clay I keep it in a tea box . With a racoon paw and a 6 toed rooster foot from beans' bad rooster.
