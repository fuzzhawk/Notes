Knife in the sink
Sink the knife in



Terror and pain
Unexpected injury
Cut through your veins
Blade slicing through your meat

Sludge in the drain
Seeping into your flesh
Infected wound
Hands are a bloody mess

Without warning
Metal beneath your skin
Your life is forfeit
When the knife sinks in
