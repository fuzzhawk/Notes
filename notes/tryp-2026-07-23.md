Fate arrived early
Ahead of the morning
Tripped on the cornerstone
Blown out of proportion
The air thick with spirit
Ancient tension
Destiny's message

Casting our yarrow stalkes
And cards
Breaking out of incarceration
Bending the bars
The bard
Swing with the umbilical pendulum
Resonance healing the scars

Reverse the ages
Intoxication and oxidization
A monsters faces
Launched into space with
A case of basic
And a handful of raisins
Medicine for the patient

We carved and we sparred
Camera catching the part I've forgotten
Blacked out drunk and still in the bar
Carry me home and I'll sing you a song of the star
Twinkle twinkle the sphinxs riddle
fictional but reality is so much more bizzare
