"Shes a teller of love "
I was in train station thinking about my dad and got sad that I would never have kids

Dreamed about me and Erin working on an ai animation with hands moving back and forth with forks and spoons. Then they were telling me that their boyfriend didn't like it and that made me angry

Then I was in someone's kitchen ( not my house ) and listening to a rap/jazz/poetry album that Julie made. It was really good. The lyrics seemed to be about Erin and other people a lot about attraction and the mechanics of romance. There was a male voice also in there doing back ups and ad libs

Then I saw Julie and was telling her how much I liked the song. Then we were outside and she disappeared into a corn field. Then beanz was there and we were laughing and running around.
