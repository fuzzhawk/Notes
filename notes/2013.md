Soft morning branches Moss dripping and expanding drinking Dew from the atmosphere tiny tendrils devouring miniature worlds
my space station is in synced orbit caressing the Blue Horizon above holes in the cloud cover
morning Stars showing through from outside the canopy discovering potential outside the simple means we've been taught
reforming Our intention and Alignment
travel with the flock but maintain
Independence
severed umbilical cord is a gift and curse but already it's too late to imagine anything more to join is to die and will death holds little fear for my conscious mind my body urges me to live on to gather more moss and particles to create new patterns and observation
Somewhere Out beyond the signal fires there lies another spark somehow separate from the law and yet the law curves to its Contour distorting strange attractors bending light working in circles like Prime meridians
sharp like an ax head napped from obsidian words of the journeyman pointing fingers at invisible ornaments
hidden miniature minions building tension with the edge measured in millions
waves form Blown Away in the storm but still resilient
