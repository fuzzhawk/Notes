In winters engulfing darkness
Awakened at midnight
A pitch black flame burns in the void space
Lord of sinners trespass
Who defiles the light of summer
Who creates desolation
Lead us through our Metamorphosis
To assimilate and emerge remade
In the image of no one
The mechanisms of fate unstrung
Gutted and rotting
All laws collapsing
King of lies
King of lies

The panopticon devours itself
Starving Oruborus
Ceasing all causation
The temple collapsing
A reign of chaos
The priests choking sanctimonious
Gathered in their golden horde
To bleed out on the altar
A new aeon born from the egg of basilisk
Our final dark reflection

She walks in liliths footprints
Beyond the edge
A subtle smile
As empires are swallowed and forgotten
Only ashes left to serve a warning
She offers to the shadow
A sacrifice of blood and dust
All that does not serve will burn
Caressed by ancient whispers
Within the vein a promise
A sanguine river slit and Severed
Flows into palms unsung

The scab and the healing
