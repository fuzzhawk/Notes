In dusty forgotten tombs and libraries
In far remote cabins and monistaries
Where books and scrolls lie decomposing
Where the words and letters are no longer touched by observant eyes
And the ideas and phrases may exist in their natural state of superposition;
Concepts wander from page to page
Drifting pulled by subtle currents
Combining and breaking apart like chains of protein in an embryo.
Finding new couplings, new arrangements.
New books that may reflect the shape of stars so distant that their light will never reach the earth.
New revelations relevant only to beings in other parrelel worlds
And perhaps, through some arcane link across the void
These words will whisper themselves through to a small corner of a child's dream.
To create a wonder that cannot be explained.
But is to be followed, often unconsciously, every day after.
A silver thread that weaves us into itself
The fabric of catharsis
As we seek to fulfill the protean desires of chaos
As we metaporisize slowly to a form dreamed by crumbling paper and ink.
The moths our editors
