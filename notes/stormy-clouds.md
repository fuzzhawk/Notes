(Wanna be)
You think that you need it

Think you want to say something
Reach it

Stripped out on the toxic box
My wires laid bare
Leaky robot with haywire heart
on a breath ensnared (on a vessel carried )

Unaddressed entanglements
And paradoxes
With broken clocks stuck in our pockets

Spouting bad news
All these colors pass through
Mind ripped into facets
Absorbed by the object

Backstage it's sleaze and constant
Feed the machine and vomit the content

But the contract broke with the construct
There's no code of conduct

Just a flood of consequences
My confessions could fill hours
My obsessions devoured

My intentions clouded
Prometheus's gift a trojan ritual
Digital artifacts poison the fountain
