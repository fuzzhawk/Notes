Dark waves lap against moonlight branches
The tide is within the forest
I am suspended in the warm night waters
Lucidity blooms into conscious
I cross the threshold.
The mountains are formed out of clay bearing the marks of a child's hand
I am in another place so similar to my home but with another keeper
I fly beyond the atmosphere and destroy the planet

In a dome so high I cannot see the ceiling
Beings from many worlds gather to exchange their knowledge

Some are shadows made of unstable particles

Some cannot be perceived by the conventions of vision

I am taught to raise my eyes upwards and jump without watching the ground below me

Falling through layers and into new realities
