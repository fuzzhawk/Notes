there's a world in dreams

there's a world in each leaf

and a world within you

a world within me

learn the verb "to be"

lemme learn to breathe freely


our existence depends on our reciprocity

the sacred exchange of information and energy

intention manifested in concrete

power beyond belief

dawn to eternity the flowers are on to me


speaking to my subgenius when I'm wandering

peaking out from behind the problems i'm pondering

giving you my blessing with my word

always forward never follow the heard


with a fist in the air turning the grey space green again

with our feet in the soul learning the process and the price of what we spend

and the deal never mentioned


the suffering grandmothers and children unborn

but we're here to renegotiate

while the people are suffering like they're the ones that made the mistake

"it's a sure supply - till it bleeds dry

egomaniac with broken reductionist logic

lonely formulating a matrix and applaud it

most people forgot it but their subconscious bought it


now their focus is frozen

and the plot is still unfolding

mountains still turning to dust and deserts to oceans

blood spilling and flowers growing

and the self is still the most potent opponent
