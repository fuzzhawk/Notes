Our lives reflected by a mirror that accelerates
Our desires.
Manifested longing become abstracted into a sculpted pitch.
Hurtling particals of fascination, slung shot to pierce our precious membranes
Every blemish and stain magnified
 exponentially exploited
to squeeze another silver drop into our most innocent absorbers
Mouths open, souls empty overfilled
Slogans leak from our tortured pores
To be wrung dry by desire

( break )

We become the embodiment of unfulfillment
Thirsty for a milk that gives no sustenance
Salivating with dry tounges
stumbling to regurgitate
parcels of witless prose
We watch as our truest form becomes revealed to us through our indifference.
Cold and cruel, unflinching as our relations crumble.
As we can no longer decern between reality and the shadows we cast on to enchanted stones with liquid lightning
Our Distorted reflections are the dreamers now, we are simply here to drool and witness.
Our every move assisted
Foretold in ledgers
 by prophets
Scribbled
in the margins

Beloved
Tear us free from this yearning
Let the hungry feast again
On fruits that blossom from our hearts freely
Unlock our silver screened cages
And let our Feathers taste once more the nameless wind
To fly forever
Release the wild children
Let them return to imperfection
