Vancouver

There is a strange stillness in this grimy city
The spirits could not be eliminated or cheapened.
Caked in grease and vomit they still stand guard under trampled streets and watch from between the shadows of the high rise
The crows perfect the art of the dive
Communing in liquid formation  above oil slicked roof tops, the stray cats and rodents plot mutiny in the cellars and sewers below.
The cedars breath in car exhaust do their best to cleanse psychic vampirism.
Children shriek in shrill harmonies with train metal.  Crow taps a rhythm on a tin roof to the dirge of the can pickers rusty cart. Rattling chains and laughter, explosions behind closed doors and curses shouted by invisible voices on abandoned streets stolen hits and kisses whiskey bottles smashed into fractal asymmetry mirrored only by my own double vision shoplifted paint fumes and lethal downtown cologne clouds of Lost Dreams slain by alarm clocks Circle over the harbor and Retreat to the watchful purple mountains the city grinds people down into powder but it's overclocked years are jammed by the blood and wooden shoes of those who refuse to be pulverized scarred Battlegrounds of countless mind Wars my thoughts spiral out I can't help but to project full Mystic cuz the alternative is to surrender into the zombie trance and risk your soul to the corporate feudal parasite gods heavy strings pulled and wool thick over eyes that burn with a caustic fire
