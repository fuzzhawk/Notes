I was awakened by the fated information
My test case in the basement
Sigil voracious
Purveyor of strange tastes
Virulent strain to your main frame
Indicated by electrical activity inside your greater brain.

Naked in the rain dancing
Questions with no answers
Balance of reality with the energy that shatters it
Paddle upstream or drift down river physical
Animistic intelligent arrows in my quiver
Let fly to the target
The path of the adept long road to meet the master
Who makes the grass green the space behind the mirror massive
As we project energy across forbidden chasms

Let the experiment commence
Finger on the pulse of the planetary dance
Let the thought form be born and condense
Extended long after the fuel has been spent
The lines of communication are bent
Curved light of a mirage
Simple methods fuel the vessel to project beyond the stars

Shielded by fields of disregard
Placebo effect send messages through the cards
Patterns of patter prepare the psyche for the charge
To rip a hole in the stoic
And dismantle the facade
