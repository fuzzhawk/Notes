Grind me down
Sharpen me to a bitter edge
I'm dull and out of life
Make me realize

Take your blade and mine
And defend what you still believe
I'm now far too gone
To sleep and to succeed

Follow
This asteroid gone out of sight
Cold and spinning through
The places all devoid of light
