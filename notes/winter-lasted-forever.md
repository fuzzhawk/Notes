Winter lasted forever
We slept beneath snowbanks
Breathing apparatus affixed to masks on our faces
Recycling oxygen
Disengaged from the sensory
Grey matter dreaming away centuries
Only essential sentience keeping our hearts beating in run-on sentences
In a drifted spiral linearity became our rival
We looped the point of origin till it became our destination
We watched our dissolution till it became our creation
And as the sentinels decomposed within corroded suits of armour
We conjured imagery of infinity
And admitted back in the monsters
We are all gone now beyond the confines of our costumes.
The flesh relased the phantom
And held reality for ransom
